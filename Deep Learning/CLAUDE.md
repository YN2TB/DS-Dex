**⚠️ THE CIPHER IS NOT A FIXED SUBSTITUTION — exactly the Mankiw hazard, found on book p. 10 (PDF p. 51).** Two adjacent sentences of the mushroom example:

- `0:21 + 0:8 0 =1` is $0.2 \times \infty + 0.8 \times 0 = \infty$ — here **`1` is `∞`**
- `0:2 0 + 0:8 1 = 0:8` is $0.2 \times 0 + 0.8 \times 1 = 0.8$ — here **`1` is a genuine 1**

Both are ASCII `0x31`. Verified by codepoint inspection. **There is no mechanical decoder — you must know the mathematics to read the formula.**

**⇒ THE RULE, same one Mankiw forced in Macro/Micro: never transcribe a formula from the extraction. Reconstruct it from the prose and the mathematics, then verify numerically against the book's own printed figures.** A deleted minus sign turns a correct equation into a plausible wrong one with no visible damage — exactly the vault's recurring "plausible wrong answer with no error" failure.

## Findings worth keeping
- **ch. 08 — THE UNIFYING RESULT OF THE WHOLE SUBJECT, AND IT IS MY SYNTHESIS, NOT D2L's: EVERY ARCHITECTURE AFTER THE MLP IS A DIFFERENT WAY OF SHORTENING OR NEUTRALISING A PRODUCT OF JACOBIANS.** ch. 04 §8: a sigmoid MLP's factor is **bounded by 0.25**, so it dies at ~11 layers. ch. 05 §14: **ResNet makes one path the identity** (gain exactly 1). ch. 07 §9: **the LSTM makes one path $\prod F_j$** (gain 1 at $F{=}1$). ch. 08 §8: **self-attention makes the path length 1, so the product has ONE term.** Quantified via ch. 07's band: admissible $\gamma$ is **±0.693% at path length 1000, ±99,900% at path length 1** ⇒ ***the constraint does not loosen, it CEASES TO EXIST.*** **That is the thread through all eight chapters and no single chapter of D2L states it.**
- **ch. 08 — THE CROSSOVER D2L PRINTS IN ITS OWN TABLE AND NEVER DIVIDES: SELF-ATTENTION IS CHEAPER THAN AN RNN IFF $n<d$.** $O(n^2d)$ vs $O(nd^2)$. **At the Transformer's $d_{model}{=}512$ self-attention wins at every sequence length below 512 tokens** — most sentences — and loses above it, which is exactly why long-context is a separate research area. D2L says only ‘prohibitively slow for very long sequences’. ⇒ ***attention did NOT win on speed: it is asymptotically WORSE past $n{=}d$. It won on path length and on $O(1)$ sequential ops (perfect parallelism).***
- **ch. 08 — ONE ZERO PRECISION ZEROES BLEU ENTIRELY, AND D2L PRINTS THE CASE WITHOUT COMMENT.** All four printed precisions reproduce (4/5, 3/4, 1/3, 0), the brevity penalty ($e^{-2}{=}0.1353$) and **all four printed translations**. **Its own run scores *soyez calmes .* against *il est calme .* as EXACTLY 0.000** — $p_2{=}0/2$, and BLEU is a **product**, so $0^{1/4}$ collapses it — although that is a valid imperative rendering of ‘he's calm’. ⇒ **BLEU measures n-gram overlap with ONE reference, not quality**; the original paper uses multiple references and corpus-level counts for exactly this. *The vault's ‘accurate number meaning something else’ pattern: the 0.000 is correctly computed and is not a measure of quality.*
- **ch. 08 — THE $1/\sqrt d$ IS A TRAINABILITY REQUIREMENT, NOT A NORMALISATION CONVENTION, AND THE CONSEQUENCE IS LEFT IMPLICIT.** $\operatorname{Var}[q^\top k]=d$ verified (510.44 at $d{=}512$). **Unscaled at $d{=}512$ the logits have sd 22.63, the max attention weight is 0.9523 and the entropy 0.117 of a possible 2.303 nats — effectively ONE-HOT.** The softmax Jacobian $p_i(\delta_{ij}-p_j)$ vanishes there. **Scaled: entropy 1.9228.** ⇒ *without it, deep attention models simply do not train, and the failure is silent — the loss just stops falling.*
- **ch. 08 — MULTI-HEAD ATTENTION IS FREE, EXACTLY.** Under D2L's own $p_q{=}p_k{=}p_v{=}p_o/h$, the projections total $4p_o^2$ **at every $h$** — verified identical for $h=1,2,4,8,16$. **The heads PARTITION one budget rather than adding to it**, so single-head at the same width is strictly worse: same cost, one similarity structure instead of $h$. *Same free-structure move as ch. 05 §15's grouped convolutions, and the same caveat: nothing crosses heads until $W_o$ mixes them.*
- **ch. 08 — ch. 05 §17's INVERSION IN A THIRD SETTING: A TRANSFORMER LAYER IS 66.7% FEED-FORWARD AND 33.3% ATTENTION.** At $d_{model}{=}512$, $d_{ffn}{=}2048$: attention **1,048,576**, FFN **2,099,712**, LayerNorm 2,048. Ratio $d_{ffn}/(2d) = $ **2.00**. Six layers = 18,902,016 params = 72.1 MB (288.4 MB to train with Adam, by ch. 04 §5). ⇒ ***the component the architecture is NAMED AFTER holds a third of its weights*** — attention is where the $O(n^2)$ COMPUTE is, the FFN is where the WEIGHTS are. **D2L gives no parameter count for the Transformer.**
- **ch. 08 — THE TRANSFORMER IS A RECOMBINATION: THE ONLY GENUINELY NEW COMPONENT IS MULTI-HEAD SELF-ATTENTION.** Residual connections ← ch. 05 §14 (and that is *why* $d_{model}$ is constant throughout: $x + \mathrm{sublayer}(x)$ requires matching dims); layer normalisation ← ch. 05 §13's batch norm, normalising over FEATURES so it has **no cross-example dependence and no batch-size sensitivity** (which matters because batch statistics over padded variable-length sequences are meaningless); the positionwise FFN ← ch. 05 §5's $1\times1$ convolution **exactly**; encoder–decoder and masking ← §2 of the same chapter. ⇒ **its power comes from REMOVING recurrence, not from adding machinery.**
- **ch. 08 — POSITIONAL ENCODING'S RELATIVE-POSITION IDENTITY VERIFIED TO $3.7\times10^{-15}$**, for every offset ($\delta{=}1,5,17$), all 16 column pairs and 60 positions: a fixed $2\times2$ rotation maps position $i$ to $i+\delta$ **independently of $i$**. Wavelengths ladder from 6.3 to 35,333 — **a continuous binary counter**, D2L's own analogy. ⇒ *the model can attend by RELATIVE position with a fixed linear map, and because the encoding is a FORMULA rather than a table it is defined past the training length.* **That is the whole justification for sinusoidal over learned embeddings.**
- **ch. 08 — GREEDY DECODING OPTIMISES THE MOST LIKELY *TOKEN*, NOT THE MOST LIKELY *SEQUENCE*, AND D2L'S EXAMPLE PROVES IT.** $0.5\cdot0.4\cdot0.4\cdot0.6 = 0.048$ vs $0.5\cdot0.3\cdot0.6\cdot0.6 = 0.054$ (both reproduced) — **the better sequence's second token had only the SECOND-highest probability.** Costs: greedy $10^5$, beam($k{=}10$) $10^6$, exhaustive $10^{40}$ ⇒ **beam is 10× greedy and $10^{34}$× cheaper than exhaustive.** *And LENGTH-NORMALISE: $\log P$ is a sum of negative terms, so unnormalised beam search always prefers the shortest candidate and truncates systematically.*
- **ch. 07 — THE NUMBER THAT EXPLAINS WHY RNNs ARE HARD, AND D2L DESCRIBES IT IN WORDS ONLY.** BPTT's gradient is $\gamma^T$ over a **reused** matrix. **For $\gamma^{1000}$ to stay inside $[10^{-3},10^{3}]$, $\gamma$ must lie in $[0.993116,\,1.006932]$ — within ±0.693% of 1** (±7.2% at T=100, ±99.5% at T=10), and D2L itself says sequences of “over a thousand tokens” are routine. **Contrast ch. 04 §8: a sigmoid MLP's factor is BOUNDED BY 0.25 so it can only vanish, and its layers have INDEPENDENT weights. An RNN's factor is unbounded in both directions AND identical every step, so there is no averaging.** ⇒ ***vanishing and exploding are ONE mechanism here, which is why RNNs needed two fixes (clipping + gating) where MLPs needed one (ReLU).***
- **ch. 07 — THE LSTM'S FIX IS ONE PARTIAL DERIVATIVE, AND THE GATE CAN NEVER REACH IT.** $\partial C_t/\partial C_{t-1} = F_t$, so the cell-path gradient is $\prod F_j$ — **exactly 1 for every $T$ when $F=1$** (the constant error carousel). **But $F$ is a sigmoid, so $F=1$ is unreachable**: at zero-bias init $F=0.5$ and the **memory half-life is ONE STEP**; $z=4$ gives 38.2 steps; **1,000-step memory needs pre-activation $z\approx7.3$.** ⇒ ***the carousel is an ASYMPTOTE the network approaches, not a switch it flips — and the gradient that would teach it to climb is the very one the forgetting destroys.*** **Hence the standard practice of initialising the forget-gate bias to +1 or +2, which D2L does not mention (it zeroes all biases).**
- **ch. 07 — D2L POSES ZIPF'S EXPONENT AS AN EXERCISE (9.2.2) AND NEVER ANSWERS IT.** Fitted from its own printed top-10 lists: **unigram 0.7184, bigram 0.5703, trigram 0.6447** ($R^2$ 0.93–0.97). Its ‘smaller exponent’ claim is **confirmed relative to unigrams and NOT monotone in $n$** → filed as declined discrepancy D9. **And the finding it sets up without stating: the top-10 unigrams are 10/10 stop words, bigrams 9/10, trigrams 1/10 — the top trigram is *the time traveller* (59) against *the* (2,261).** ⇒ ***signal improves with $n$ exactly as frequency collapses: the information lives where the counts run out.*** That is the real argument against count-based models, and D2L makes it about storage only.
- **ch. 07 — THE EXPONENT D2L NAMES WITHOUT A NUMBER.** An $n$-gram model stores $|\mathcal V|^n$ counts: for The Time Machine's **28 characters** that is 21,952 at $n{=}3$, $1.72\times10^7$ at $n{=}5$, and **$2.96\times10^{14}$ = 1.05 PB at $n{=}10$**; for a 10,000-word vocabulary $n{=}3$ alone is **3.64 TB**. **The RNN is 2,876 parameters at ANY context length.** ⇒ *the RNN's advantage is not accuracy, it is that its cost is $O(1)$ in context where counting is $O(|\mathcal V|^n)$.*
- **ch. 07 — D2L'S EXERCISE 10.2.3 ANSWERED: CELL COSTS ARE EXACTLY 1 : 3 : 4, AT EVERY SIZE.** One gate/state block is $dh+h^2+h$; **RNN 1 block, GRU 3 ($R,Z,\tilde H$), LSTM 4 ($I,F,O,\tilde C$)** — structural, not empirical. Deep: $+(L-1)(2h^2+h)$. **Bidirectional: 1.99–2.00×, so a bidirectional LSTM is 8× a vanilla RNN's recurrent cost.** *Worth knowing before reaching for the most elaborate cell by default.*
- **ch. 07 — PERPLEXITY'S UPPER BOUND IS THE VOCABULARY SIZE, AND ITS SCALE FLATTERS PROGRESS.** Anchors 1 / $|\mathcal V|$ / $\infty$; here **28**. In bits: **4.8074 uniform vs Shannon's ≈1.1 for real English (ppl 2.14)** ⇒ **the entire usable range is 13.1× in perplexity but only 3.7 bits.** A model at ppl 10 sounds 64% better than uniform and has covered **40%** of the available bits. ⇒ ***report perplexity WITH the vocabulary size, and convert to bits to compare*** — character- and word-level perplexities are not comparable at all.
- **ch. 07 — THE GRU TIES WHAT THE LSTM LEAVES FREE, and that is the whole structural difference.** LSTM: $C_t = F_t\odot C_{t-1} + I_t\odot\tilde C_t$ with **two independent gates** — it can forget without writing. GRU: $H_t = Z_t\odot H_{t-1} + (1-Z_t)\odot\tilde H_t$ is a **convex combination**, so forgetting and writing are one knob. ⇒ **25% fewer parameters for one lost degree of freedom** — and *if a task needs to clear state without absorbing the current input, that is a STRUCTURAL argument for the LSTM, not a hyperparameter search.*
- **ch. 07 — BIDIRECTIONAL RNNs CANNOT PREDICT THE FUTURE, and it is the chapter's easiest catastrophic mistake because it produces EXCELLENT validation numbers.** They condition on tokens after $t$, so for language modelling the answer is in the input (leakage) and for forecasting the future does not exist yet. **Correct for tagging, NER and masked-token tasks — BERT's setting, and exactly not GPT's.** *D2L's §9.1 remark that ‘the forward direction is easier than the reverse’ is the same observation from the other side.*
- **ch. 07 — CLIPPING IS EXACT, BIASED, AND ONLY HALF A FIX.** $g \leftarrow \min(1,\theta/\|g\|)g$: the norm never exceeds $\theta$, **gradients below $\theta$ are UNTOUCHED** (not shrinkage), and the direction is exactly preserved. Via Lipschitz, it bounds one step's effect on the objective — **an unclipped norm-1000 gradient at $\eta{=}0.1$ permits the objective to move by 100; clipping at 1 caps it at 0.1.** **D2L is blunt: ‘To be clear, it is a hack.’** ⇒ **it cannot rescale a gradient that has underflowed to zero, so the vanishing half needs the architecture, not the optimiser.**
- **ch. 06 — THE OMISSION THAT MATTERS MOST, AND IT TOOK REGENERATING THE SOURCE'S OWN ANCHOR SET TO SEE IT: SSD's FOREGROUND/BACKGROUND IMBALANCE IS 123:1 AT BEST AND 5,443:1 FOR A SMALL OBJECT, AND D2L's LOSS ADDRESSES ONLY HALF OF IT.** Built TinySSD's full **5,444**-anchor set (verified against the printed count) and labelled it against five ground-truth boxes: medium object **123:1**, D2L's own dog box **453:1**, a 10%-wide object **5,443:1**. **The `bbox_masks` variable zeroes the OFFSET loss for negatives — D2L says so explicitly — and the CLASS loss is plain cross-entropy summed over all 5,444 anchors.** ⇒ **a “background everywhere” predictor scores 99.8% of the available class accuracy.** Focal Loss (Lin et al. 2017), hard-negative mining and sampling ratios all exist for exactly this and **none appears in the chapter**. *Vault pattern: the plausible-wrong-answer-with-no-error — training converges, loss falls, detector detects nothing.*
- **ch. 06 — THE IoU THRESHOLD IS NOT A DETAIL, IT IS THE TRAINING SIGNAL.** Same 50%-wide box, varying only the threshold: 0.3 → **203 positives (26:1)**; 0.5 → 32 (169:1); 0.6 → 12 (453:1); 0.7 → **4 (1,360:1)**. **Moving 0.6→0.3 multiplies positives 16.9×.** *(Best IoU any anchor reaches with that box is 0.7693, so at 0.8 the threshold pass yields NOTHING and only the algorithm's step-2 guarantee saves it.)* ⇒ **report the positive:negative ratio before trusting any detection loss.**
- **ch. 06 — IoU 0.5 IS NOT “HALF OVERLAPPING”, AND IoU CANNOT SEPARATE TWO DIFFERENT ERRORS.** For two unit squares offset by $d$, $\mathrm{IoU} = (1-d)/(1+d)$, so **IoU 0.5 needs $d = 1/3$ exactly — they share two-thirds of their width**; the everyday “50% overlap” ($d{=}0.5$) scores only 0.3333. **Because the union grows as the intersection shrinks, IoU falls about twice as fast as intuition expects.** And **IoU 0.5 is reachable by SHIFTING or by NESTING** (a box of half the area inside, side $\sqrt2$ — verified exactly 0.500000) ⇒ *it collapses “right size, wrong place” and “right place, wrong size” into one number, and gives **zero gradient for any disjoint pair**. (That degeneracy is why GIoU/DIoU exist.)*
- **ch. 06 — THE VAULT'S BEST TENSOR-LEVEL VERIFICATION SO FAR: ALL THREE PRINTED LABELLING TENSORS REGENERATED FROM FIVE ANCHOR BOXES AND TWO GROUND-TRUTH BOXES ALONE.** Classes `[0,1,2,0,2]`, the 20-element mask, and **all twenty offsets** to the three significant figures D2L prints. Also reproduced: the NMS output, **both** RoI-pooling outputs, the 2,042,040 and 5,444 anchor counts, the 25,300 concatenation and **all five geometric-mean scales**. ⇒ **D2L §14.1–14.8 is the cleanest source range met in this subject — every checkable number was correct and NO discrepancy was found.**
- **ch. 06 — THE 375× REDUCTION THAT IS THE WHOLE POINT OF MULTISCALE DETECTION, AND D2L STATES BOTH NUMBERS PAGES APART WITHOUT DIVIDING.** Per-pixel anchors on 561×728: **2,042,040** (= 38.9 MB of LABELS for one image). TinySSD's five feature-map levels: **5,444**. Like-for-like at 256×256 it is still 60×, and **the feature-map budget is 48× smaller at EVERY anchors-per-unit setting because $a$ cancels** ⇒ ***the saving comes from sampling POSITIONS, not shapes*** — which is why the $n{+}m{-}1$ trick (44.4%) is a footnote and the pyramid is the chapter. **And the count is not the main point: each level's receptive field assigns it an object size** (51–70 px at 32×32, 225–246 px at 1×1).
- **ch. 06 — NMS CONVERTS A FALSE POSITIVE INTO A FALSE NEGATIVE AND THE THRESHOLD *IS* THAT TRADE** (D2L's exercise 14.4.4, unanswered). Reproduced exactly on its example. **Constructed the failure: two genuinely distinct objects whose boxes sit within 30% of a box-width score IoU 0.5385 and the lower-confidence one is DELETED, unrecoverably** — two people side by side, cars in a queue, cells in a smear. **NMS uses only geometry and cannot distinguish ‘two boxes on one object’ from ‘two objects’.** Soft-NMS decays instead of deleting, keeping the decision reversible.
- **ch. 06 — THE R-CNN FAMILY IS ONE MOVE REPEATED, AND IT IS ch. 05's ARCHITECTURE HISTORY AGAIN: DELETE A HAND-DESIGNED STAGE.** Fast R-CNN is **2,000× fewer CNN forward passes** than R-CNN (2,000 → 1); Faster R-CNN then deletes selective search itself, which is what finally makes the detector end-to-end trainable. **SIFT/SURF/HOG → learned kernels (AlexNet); fc head → global average pooling (NiN); selective search → region proposal network; and then anchors+NMS → set prediction (DETR, 2020).** ⇒ ***this chapter's two most intricate components are the ones the field deleted next.*** *‘Contains a neural network’ and ‘is trained end to end’ are different claims.*
- **ch. 06 — THE ASSIGNMENT ALGORITHM'S MOST IMPORTANT LINE IS THE ONE D2L PRESENTS WITHOUT COMMENT.** Step 2 guarantees every ground-truth box an anchor **before** the threshold applies — which is why $A_1$ is labelled ‘dog’ at an IoU of **0.1417**, far below 0.5. **Without that guarantee a small or oddly-shaped object would have NO positive anchor and could never be learned.** *Also: the offsets are standardized ($\times10$ on centres, $\times5$ on log sizes) so one $\ell_1$ loss weights all four sensibly — and the decode MUST invert it, or you get boxes in the right place with the wrong size.*
- **ch. 06 — SSD's HEADS ARE CONVOLUTIONS BECAUSE THE ALTERNATIVE IS 116,308× MORE PARAMETERS** — ch. 05 §17's inversion doing useful work. One 64-channel 32×32 map with $a{=}4$, $q{=}1$: **536,879,104** parameters as a fully connected head vs **4,616** as a $3\times3$ convolution. **All five of TinySSD's detection heads together are 124,536 parameters — under 1/800 of VGG-11's single first fc layer.**
- **ch. 06 — FINE-TUNING'S OPERATIONAL CONTENT IS A LEARNING-RATE ASYMMETRY, and D2L's own baseline confirms it.** Pretrained layers get the base rate, the fresh head gets **10×** — and the from-scratch comparison run uses **exactly the head's rate** ($5\times10^{-4}$, i.e. 10× the fine-tuned base). ⇒ **the pretrained weights are nearly right so you nudge them; the head is random so you train it. One learning rate for both destroys the backbone in the first few hundred steps.** *Works only because of ch. 05's stem/body/head factorization.*
- **ch. 05 — THE ORGANIZING RESULT OF THE SUBJECT SO FAR, AND D2L NEVER TABULATES IT: PARAMETERS AND COMPUTATION LIVE IN OPPOSITE HALVES OF EVERY PRE-2013 CNN.** LeNet **4.2% of params / 85.9% of FLOPs** in the convolutions; AlexNet **8.0% / 95.4%**; VGG-11 **7.2% / 98.4%**. Cause in one line: a conv weight is reused at all $h\times w$ positions so its FLOPs are $h\cdot w$ times its parameter count, while **an fc layer's FLOPs-per-parameter is EXACTLY 1.0** (verified for every fc layer in all three nets). ⇒ ***'convolutional neural network' describes where the arithmetic is, not where the weights are*** — and it explains the whole architectural history: **to shrink a net you attack the head (NiN), to speed it up you attack the convolutions (grouped conv, 1×1 bottlenecks). Different problems, different layers.**
