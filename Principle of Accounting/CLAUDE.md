## GAAP vs IFRS

The course mixes **US GAAP and IAS 1 vocabulary** — Balance Sheet / Statement of Financial Position, Income Statement / Statement of Profit or Loss. Two substantive divergences, both flagged in the notes:

- **LIFO is permitted under GAAP but prohibited under IFRS** — examinable, but not applicable in Vietnam
- **Revaluation is permitted under IFRS but prohibited under GAAP**
