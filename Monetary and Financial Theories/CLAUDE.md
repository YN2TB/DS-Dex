## ⚠️ EXTRACTION — tested, and it is MILDER than Macro/Micro's

**Mishkin does NOT share Mankiw's operator cipher.** Inline arithmetic extracts correctly: `$1 million/(1 + 0.10) = $909,090` comes through with real `+`, `=`, `/`. **The never-transcribe rule does not apply in its strong form here.**

**⚠️ BUT displayed equations lose their parentheses to digits:**

| extracts as | means |
|---|---|
| `11 + i2` | $(1+i)$ |
| `11 + i22` | $(1+i)^2$ |
| `11 + i2n` | $(1+i)^n$ |

**So `(` → `1`, `)` → `2`, with any exponent appended immediately after the closing digit.** The YTM formula extracts as `LV = FP/11 + i2 + FP/11 + i22 + ... + FP/11 + i2n`, i.e. $LV=\sum FP/(1+i)^t$.

**⚠️ And in INLINE maths the parentheses survive but superscripts flatten**: `(1 + 0.10)2` means $(1.10)^2$, not $\times 2$.
